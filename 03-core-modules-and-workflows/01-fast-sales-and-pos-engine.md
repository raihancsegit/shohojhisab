# Fast Sales & POS Engine

## 1. The 3-Tap Checkout Workflow

```
[1. Scan Barcode / Voice / Tap Quick Tile]
               ▼
[2. Choose Customer or Instant Cash]
               ▼
[3. Tap CONFIRM (বিক্রি সম্পন্ন)]
```

---

## 2. Key Sales Features
* **Smart Barcode Camera Scanner:** Built directly into mobile camera with continuous scanning mode.
* **Fractional Quantity Input:** Full support for loose weight items (e.g. 1.250 kg sugar, 0.5 meter wire).
* **Split Payment:** Customer pays ৳500 cash, ৳300 via bKash, and ৳200 recorded as customer due (বাকি).
* **Thermal Receipt & SMS Memo:** Connects via Bluetooth to 58mm/80mm mini thermal printer. Sends free SMS e-memo to customer phone.
