# Expenses & Real-Time Profit Engine

## 1. Automated Profit Formula

$$\text{Gross Profit} = \sum (\text{Selling Price} - \text{Purchase Price}) \times \text{Quantity Sold}$$

$$\text{Net Profit} = \text{Gross Profit} - \text{Daily Expenses (দোকান ভাড়া + বিদ্যুৎ + চা/নাস্তা + স্টাফ বেতন)}$$

---

## 2. Cash Drawer Reconciliation (ক্যাশ ড্রয়ার মেলানো)
At the end of the day, the shopkeeper enters actual cash in hand. The system compares it with calculated sales and displays exact surplus/shortage.
