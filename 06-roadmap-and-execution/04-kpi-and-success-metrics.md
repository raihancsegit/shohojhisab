# North Star KPIs & Success Metrics

## 1. Core Product Metrics
* **North Star Metric:** **Daily Active Transacting Shops (DATS)** — Number of shops completing at least 5 transactions/day.
* **30-Day Retention Rate:** Target > 75% active usage after 1 month.
* **Trial-to-Paid Conversion:** Target > 25% conversion after free 30-day pilot.
* **Monthly Churn Rate:** Keep below 4% per month.

---

## 2. Operational Health Metrics
* **Offline Sync Success Rate:** > 99.8% conflict-free syncs.
* **Average POS Checkout Time:** < 5 seconds per transaction.
* **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Growth:** 20% month-over-month.
