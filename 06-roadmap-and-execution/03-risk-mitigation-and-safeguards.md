# Risk Mitigation & Safeguards

| Risk Identified | Severity | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopkeeper Abandons Software** | High | Keep sales flow to 3 taps. Send nightly WhatsApp summary to keep owner engaged. |
| **Cloud SMS Costs Exceed Subscription** | Medium | Use WhatsApp notifications where possible (cheaper/richer) and cap free SMS to 50/month on Starter tier. |
| **Offline Sync Conflicts** | High | Use client-generated UUIDs and append-only financial ledger records with idempotent server processing. |
| **Phone Loss / Damage by Shopkeeper** | Medium | Continuous encrypted cloud backups every time phone connects to internet; 1-click restore on new phone. |
