## 2. Payment & Renewal Mechanisms
* **Direct bKash / Nagad Recurring Billing:** In-app 1-tap subscription payment via bKash Tokenized Checkout and Nagad Direct Pay.
* **Local Market Field Agent Collection:** Field agents collect yearly ৳999 cash in hand and give instant digital scratch coupons / direct account activation.
* **Grace Period Safeguard:** 7-day grace period when a subscription lapses, allowing offline sales to continue without locking shopkeepers out in middle of transactions.
