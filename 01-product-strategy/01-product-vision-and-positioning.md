# Product Vision & Positioning

## 1. The Core Problem
Over 85% of retail stores in rural and semi-urban Bangladesh manage their business using paper notebooks (লাল খাতা / বাকি খাতা):
* **Lost Revenue in Due Collection:** Forgotten customer dues and disputes over unreadable manual notes.
* **Capital Trapped in Stock:** Shopkeepers do not know which products are expiring, dead stock, or fast-moving.
* **Time-Consuming End-of-Day Tallying:** Spending 1-2 hours every night calculating cash vs dues.
* **Why existing software fails:** Existing ERPs/POS are either too expensive (৳1,000–৳5,000/mo), require complex accounting knowledge (Double entry, Debit/Credit), or require constant high-speed internet.

---

## 2. Our Mission & Vision
> **Mission:** "To empower every local shopkeeper in Bangladesh with an effortless digital assistant that automates sales, stock, dues, and profits."

### Core Philosophy: The Zero-Friction Rule
* If an action takes more than **3 taps**, redesign it.
* Never ask for manual calculation (the software calculates profit, dues, and balances automatically).
* Speak the merchant's language: **জমা (Cash In), খরচ (Expense), বাকি (Due), স্টক (Stock), লাভ (Profit)**.

## 3. Product Positioning Matrix

| Factor | Traditional Paper Khata | Existing POS / Accounting Software | **Local Business OS** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free (paper cost) | ৳800 – ৳3,500/month | **৳49 – ৳199/month** |
| **Learning Curve** | Zero | High (Needs training) | **Zero (Intuitive Bangla UI + Voice)** |
| **Internet Dependency** | None | 100% Online | **100% Offline-First (SQLite Local)** |
| **Industry Customization** | Manual | Generic / Rigid | **Dynamic Category Modules** |
| **Speed per Sale** | 30–60 seconds | 20–40 seconds | **3–5 seconds (Barcode / Voice / Quick Tap)** |
| **Automatic Insights** | None | Complex reports | **Instant Daily WhatsApp/SMS Summary** |
