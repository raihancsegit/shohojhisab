# Industry Engine: Grocery & Super Shop

* **Pre-seeded Catalog:** 5,000+ top FMCG products in Bangladesh with barcodes and images.
* **Loose Commodity Quick Numpad:** One-tap tiles for Sugar, Rice, Oil, Potato, Onion with quick weight calculation.
* **Batch Purchase Discount:** Buy 1 carton (24 pcs) at wholesale price, sell individually at retail price with auto unit break.
